Privacy Policy & Disclosures

Effective Date: January 2026

1. Privacy & Data Collection Overview

BokitCord is engineered as a privacy-respecting educational web application. We do not sell your personal data, conduct cross-site fingerprinting, or harvest identity metrics.

2. Local Browser Storage & Cookies

Our platform uses standard client-side browser storage (localStorage and session tokens) solely to maintain your authentication status, active chat session, custom avatars, and code drafts in the sandbox editor.

3. Zero-Knowledge Client-Side Encryption

Direct messages and private channels utilize Telegram-style AES-256-GCM encryption with browser-generated cryptographic keys. Plaintext message bodies and encryption keys are never stored unencrypted on our servers.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Monetization

To support ongoing server hosting, bandwidth costs, and emulator runtime maintenance, BokitCord partners with verified third-party advertising networks including Monetag.

💡 Ad Experience Guarantee

Advertisements on BokitCord are strictly confined to preroll overlays when loading games. Chat channels, developer tools, and navigation are 100% ad-free.

5. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy or advertising partnerships, contact the administrator at admin@bokitware.org.
